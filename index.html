Partner models

Pick the structure that fits how you sell.

White-label

We work under your letterhead.

You own the client. We operate as your subcontracted finance desk. You bill the client; we bill you. Useful when you want a premium service line without building the capability in-house.

Direct referral

Send the lead. We scope, bill, and run the engagement. You receive a revenue-based referral fee after the client signs and pays. Simple paper trail. No delivery load on your staff.

The work

What your clients actually receive.

FP&A

Budgets, rolling forecasts, scenario models, cash visibility, and a monthly pack owners can use in operating meetings.

Fractional CFO

Pricing and margin work, KPI cadence, lender or investor reporting, and decision support that sits above compliance.

Analytics

Cleanup, dashboards, trend work, and a short list of operating metrics that show where the business is actually moving.

Special projects

13-week cash, fundraising models, lender packages, M&A support, and one-off analysis that does not justify a hire.

How it works

A short path from intro to delivery.

  1. Send the intro

    Share the client and whether you want a referral or a white-label engagement.

  2. Fit call and scope

    We confirm goals, timing, price, and who faces the client.

  3. Paper and kickoff

    Agreements are signed, source files are collected, and work starts.

  4. Deliver and report

    Client-ready files go back through you. Communication stays tight so nothing lands sideways.
